About Me

I am a passionate Computer Science student from Bel Air, Mauritius, with a strong interest in technology, programming, web development, robotics, and digital media. My academic journey began at Camp de Masque State College, followed by Sir Leckraz Teelock State Secondary School (SLTSSS). I am currently pursuing a three-year, full-time BSc (Hons) in Computer Science at the University of Mauritius (UoM), where I continue to expand my knowledge in software development, problem-solving, and emerging technologies while preparing for a career in the technology industry.

My journey into technology began when I was 14, when I was first introduced to Arduino and robotics. What started with simple microcontroller projects soon grew into a passion for programming and software development. I later explored front-end web development, learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and have since built multiple responsive websites. One of my most rewarding projects was designing and developing the official website for SLTSSS. I enjoy turning ideas into practical and user-friendly digital solutions, and I am always eager to learn new technologies and improve my skills.

Alongside programming, I have a strong passion for photography, videography, and digital content creation. I served as the President of the SLT Press Club, where I documented numerous school events through photography and videography while leading the club’s activities. I am experienced in video editing using DaVinci Resolve and have produced a wide range of videos for the school. I also had the opportunity to edit and design the entire SLTSSS 2026 school magazine, combining creativity with technical skills to produce a professional publication.

Beyond technology and media, I am an enthusiastic astrophotography enthusiast who enjoys capturing the beauty of the night sky. I am fascinated by astronomy and love photographing the Moon, stars, planets, and deep-sky objects whenever conditions allow. Astrophotography has strengthened my patience, attention to detail, and appreciation for science, while complementing my interests in technology and photography. Looking ahead, I aspire to continue developing my skills in Computer Science and contribute to innovative projects as a developer within Oracle Club, with the long-term goal of building a successful career in the technology industry.
