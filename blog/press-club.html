Building the SLT Press Club: From an Idea to a School Community

August 2026

← Back to Blog

In 2023, while I was in Grade 11 at Sir Leckraz Teelock State Secondary School (SLTSSS), I wanted to become more involved in school life by joining a club. At the time, the school had a few clubs, including a robotics club and a chess club, but most of them were not very active. Although a Press Club had existed in the past, it did not have a strong online presence and there was no platform where students could easily follow school events. I saw an opportunity to create something that would not only document school activities but also connect the school community through social media.

In February 2023, I began planning the idea of restarting the Press Club with a modern approach. My vision was simple: create a team that would showcase everything happening at the school, from academic achievements to sports competitions, cultural celebrations, and extracurricular activities. In May 2023, we officially launched the SLT Press Club Instagram page. Since most students were already using Instagram, it became the perfect platform to share event coverage quickly and reach a large audience.

The club started with only five members, but we were determined to build something meaningful. As the club expanded, we organised ourselves into different departments. The Authors were responsible for writing articles and event reports, the Photography Team captured memorable moments during school activities, the Editors designed graphics, edited photographs, and refined articles before publication, while the Management Team coordinated projects and ensured everything ran smoothly. This structure allowed every member to contribute according to their interests and talents while working together as one team.

Throughout 2023 and 2024, the Press Club covered numerous school events, including Sports Day, Music Day, Independence Day celebrations, academic competitions, and many other activities involving students and staff. Our Instagram page steadily gained recognition within the school community, growing to hundreds of followers and becoming a trusted source of school news and updates. By the end of 2024, the page had attracted over 500 followers and continued to grow as more students became involved.

To take the project further, I began developing the official SLT Press Club website in late 2024. My goal was to create a professional digital platform where every club member could have an account and publish their own articles. The website also included dedicated sections for school news, upcoming events, other student clubs, school history, and featured articles. It was designed to become a central hub for everything happening at SLTSSS while giving students the opportunity to develop writing and digital publishing skills.

Unfortunately, in 2025, following a change in the school’s management, the website project was no longer continued and development came to an end. Although that chapter closed, the Press Club itself continued to thrive. The Instagram page kept growing and today has built a strong online community with hundreds of followers. When I completed my studies at SLTSSS, I handed the club over to a new generation of students, confident that they would continue its mission and help it grow even further.

Looking back, one of the achievements I am most proud of is seeing how the Press Club became an important part of school life. In the beginning, we received limited support, but over time teachers and staff began actively sharing information and photographs from school events with us. The Press Club evolved into an effective communication platform that helped keep students informed while celebrating the many achievements and activities taking place throughout the school. I hope that one day the website project can be revived, allowing the Press Club to continue expanding and inspiring future students to share their stories.
