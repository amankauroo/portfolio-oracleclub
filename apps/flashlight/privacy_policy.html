Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

Flashlight is a simple app that turns your device's camera flash on and off. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does, and does not, collect.

Information We Collect

Flashlight does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not connect to the internet.

Permissions

The app requests access to the device's camera solely to control the flash (torch) hardware. The camera is never used to capture photos or videos, and no images are recorded or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Flashlight does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

Flashlight does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as no data is collected at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is ever updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
